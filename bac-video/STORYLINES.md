# Storylines — "Bac AI PDF" (vertical 1080×1920, ~55 s, Darija VO + music)

**Built: A — The paper gets decoded** (recommended: one object the viewer knows — a Bac exam paper — travels from chaos to a single PDF, so the story reads even muted.)

## A. The paper gets decoded (BUILT)
Device: one object across worlds. Form: object-to-object chain, colour-flood seams.
A tower of revision pages buries the screen; "STOP". Exam papers from every branch fly into an AI scanner, the scan line finds repeated questions, pattern lines and the red "trap", chaos snaps into order, everything folds into one PDF, the PDF becomes the buy button.
First 2 s: giant "صحاب الباك!" on lime, a stack of pages slamming in under it.
Beats: hook → the old way (memorise everything) → collect every sujet → AI scan → repeats / patterns / trap → chaos→order → one PDF → CTA.
Look: full colour fields (lime / cobalt / signal red / paper), Alexandria 900 Arabic display, paper objects with hard offset shadows.
Sound: 120 BPM tech-pop bed under Darija VO; whooshes on world changes, pops on stamps, one riser into the PDF reveal.

## B. Read the examiner's mind
Device: a question with the input. Form: an endless zoom into an exam question that opens onto the next world. Bolder, less literal; harder to read muted.

## C. Before / after split
Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one — left the student drowning in notebooks, right the student with the PDF — collapsing into the PDF. Clear but familiar.
